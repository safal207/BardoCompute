# v0.29 Recovery-Readiness False-Positive Attribution — result note

Hosted Python 3.11 and 3.12 agree on the main attribution:

- 100% of failed RATE_RELAXED challenges occurred in active-stress phases.
- 0% of failed RATE_RELAXED challenges occurred in recovery/drain.
- successful RATE_RELAXED attempts had median LOAD ≈ 0.34 on both runtimes;
- failed attempts had median LOAD ≈ 0.54 on both runtimes;
- low PAIN alone was not evidence of recovery: failed attempts frequently had PAIN = 0 while the subsequent RATE-relaxed epoch missed substantial work.

This is diagnostic evidence only. The v0.28 seeds are spent and may not be used for threshold tuning or policy promotion.

Narrow interpretation:

> Supported symptom relief is not the same as transition-regime recovery. On this spent workload family, the frozen recovery evidence often declared RATE-withdrawal readiness while active stress was still present.
